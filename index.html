01 Our Story

Made with Passion.
Served with Warmth.

At Friences Café & Patisserie, we believe that the simplest moments can become the sweetest memories. From freshly baked pastries and beautifully crafted cakes to comforting coffee and a welcoming café atmosphere, every detail is thoughtfully created to bring people together.

Our inspiration comes from traditional European baking — slow fermentations, honest ingredients, and recipes polished by hand. Paired with a warm, softly lit café, it is a place to pause, to share, and to savour the little things.

  • Traditional baking inspiration
  • Carefully selected ingredients
  • A slower, warmer café experience
